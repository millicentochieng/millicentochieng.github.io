I am a Research Scientist at Microsoft Research Africa and a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the University of the Witwatersrand, Johannesburg. My work centers on multilingual and multicultural AI, with a broader goal of building AI systems that are more linguistically representative, culturally aligned and equitable.

At Microsoft Research Africa I spearhead multilingual tokenization research, developing approaches that improve linguistic representation and downstream model efficiency across global languages. I also lead work on multicultural AI alignment, examining how multilingual and multimodal systems reflect cultural context, and how community-grounded evaluation can surface misalignment across diverse underrepresented communities.

My PhD research focuses on cultural alignment in multilingual language models for African languages, supervised by Prof. Bruce Bassett. I study how regional and cultural variation can be represented, modelled and evaluated through region-conditioned data, LLM alignment methods and pluralistic evaluation.

I have published in leading NLP and AI venues including EMNLP, NAACL and LREC, among them IrokoBench, which received an Outstanding Paper Award at NAACL 2025. Alongside research I organize and lead across the African NLP and data science communities through AfricaNLP, Masakhane and WiDS. More on that here.

Long-term goal

To develop multilingual AI systems that preserve linguistic and cultural meaning while improving representation, efficiency and alignment across diverse communities, toward more equitable AI.

Before Microsoft

I completed an MSc in Data Science at the African Centre of Excellence in Data Science, University of Rwanda, where my research focused on transfer learning for precision agriculture. I later conducted research at Harvard University, applying machine learning, computer vision and statistical methods to sustainable agriculture. Earlier I led a team of 10 as a Content Supervisor for the 2019 Kenya Census at the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics, and worked as a Statistician Intern at the Kenya Ports Authority.
