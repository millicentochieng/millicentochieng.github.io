I am a Research Scientist at Microsoft Research Africa and a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the University of the Witwatersrand, supervised by Prof. Bruce Bassett. My research focuses on multilingual and multicultural AI.

I currently spearhead work on multilingual tokenization to improve linguistic representation and downstream model efficiency across global languages. In parallel, I lead work on cultural alignment, examining how models capture cultural variation across low-resource settings.

I have published at EMNLP, NAACL, LREC and ACL — including IrokoBench, which received an Outstanding Paper Award at NAACL 2025. I actively help shape the African NLP research community through Masakhane and as a workshop organizer for AfricaNLP, and I serve as WiDS Kenya Lead Ambassador, leading a community of 400+ members advocating for gender diversity in AI.

Long-term goal

To develop multilingual AI systems that preserve linguistic and cultural meaning while improving representation, efficiency and alignment across diverse communities — toward more equitable AI.

Before Microsoft

I was a research intern at the Institute for Applied Computational Science at Harvard University, applying transfer learning and computer vision to bean disease classification for sustainable agriculture. Earlier I led a team of 10 as a Content Supervisor for the 2019 Kenya Census at the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics, and worked as a Statistician Intern at the Kenya Ports Authority.
