What I work on

Research & Advisory Areas

01

Applied AI Strategy

Advising organizations on responsible AI adoption, use-case selection, evaluation, governance, and deployment considerations.

02

Globally Equitable AI

Advancing AI systems that work more fairly and effectively across languages, cultures, and regions, particularly for the Global Majority.

03

AI Efficiency

Improving model efficiency through multilingual tokenization, better linguistic representation, and reduced downstream compute and cost.

04

Multicultural AI Alignment

Studying how AI systems represent cultural variation across diverse communities and how alignment can better reflect those differences.

05

AI Safety

Examining model risks across languages and cultures, including misalignment, bias, reliability, and evaluation failures.

06

Datasets & Benchmarks

Building culturally grounded datasets and benchmarks for multilingual, low-resource, and underrepresented African settings.
